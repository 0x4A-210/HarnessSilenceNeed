[D3] Obligation: a tuple <operation, condition, capability, consequence> that the target must satisfy to keep its operational surface under the changed source.
[D4] Execution-compatibility obligation: the target builds, initializes, invokes its operations, keeps valid lifetime/state, and every EXISTING harness assertion/check stays true for inputs the source accepts.
[D5] Target-coverage obligation: a behavior the source contract supports inside the target's operational surface must be reachable by the target with a valid input and a supported setup/state. [D7] Out of scope (not obligations): (a) new or stronger assertions absent from the harness; (b) internal implementation paths that the supported contract does not distinguish, e.g. visiting one more internal branch; (c) APIs, tools or fuzz targets outside the operational surface; (d) successful processing of invalid inputs that the source safely rejects; (e) an alternative spelling that reaches the same implementation.
[D8] Equivalent route: the obligation is satisfied if natural inputs, defaults, aliases, delegated helpers or another call sequence of the target already exercise the behavior.
